How NaijaFix Works

Helping residents make their communities better in three simple steps.

01. Report an Issue

Identify a problem in your neighbourhood and submit a report with its location and description.

02. Track Progress

Follow your report and see when community officers review and assign the issue.

03. Get It Resolved

Workers attend to assigned issues and update their progress until the problem is resolved.
